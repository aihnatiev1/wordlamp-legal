Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Wordlamp ("the app", "we") is a Bible word search game.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers.

Where your progress is stored

Solved puzzles, hints used and your settings are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app only learns from Apple whether you own Wordlamp Complete. We never receive your name, email or payment details.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash statistics. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
