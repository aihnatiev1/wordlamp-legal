Common questions

How do I select a word? Drag across the letters, or tap the first letter and then the last. Words can run forwards, backwards and diagonally in later levels.

I bought Wordlamp Complete on another device. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID. Family Sharing members get it automatically.

Which Bible translation is used? The World English Bible by default, or the King James Version (switch in Settings). Both are in the public domain.

My progress isn't on my new iPhone. In version 1.0 progress is kept on the device only. Your purchase always restores.
